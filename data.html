Dataset at a glance

How the data were collected and cleaned

  • Collection: mix proportions, curing conditions and test results were extracted from published papers on fiber-reinforced cementitious and geopolymer composites (ECC/SHCC, UHPC, UHP-ECC, alkali-activated). Every value keeps a pointer to the table or page it came from; unit conversions (kg/m³ → ratio to binder) are recorded, and DOIs were checked against Crossref.
  • Training file: 04 Dataset_web.xlsx has only the columns the calculator has: the 21 materials (cement + fly ash + GGBS as the binder, the rest per binder, fibers in vol%), curing method, temperature, duration, test age, and the three measured strengths.
  • Nothing is deleted: each row is only flagged as used or not used for each model, with a reason.
  • Excluded from the models: mixes without fibers, special tests (fire, freeze–thaw, impact), rows missing the water/binder ratio or test age, statistical outliers, and materials without a reliable density. Tensile strain capacity uses direct (uniaxial / dog-bone) tension tests only.
