How the comparison was run

  • Data: mixes from published papers on fibre-reinforced cementitious and geopolymer composites (ECC/SHCC, UHPC, UHP-ECC), cleaned and screened for special test conditions, outliers and missing densities.
  • Inputs (33): only what the calculator can supply: binder shares, ratios to binder, fibre volumes, curing and test age.
  • Three ways of testing: 10-fold cross-validation with a random split (headline score: mixes shuffled, split into 10 parts, each part predicted by a model trained on the other 9), 50/50 per paper (your lab's mixes are already in the data), and paper held out (a brand-new lab, the strictest test). Each was repeated with 3 random seeds and averaged.
